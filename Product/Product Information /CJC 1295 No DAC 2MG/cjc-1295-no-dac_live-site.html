Scientific overview

CJC‑1295 (No DAC) / Mod GRF 1‑29 is a truncated GHRH analog focused on the active N‑terminal 1‑29 region, engineered to resist rapid enzymatic breakdown in research systems.[web:143][web:319][web:324]

By omitting the DAC moiety, this version supports short, controllable GH pulses that more closely resemble natural hypothalamic signalling.[web:152][web:322][web:324]

Mechanistic themes in research

GHRH receptor activation

In laboratory models, Mod GRF 1‑29 binds GHRH receptors on pituitary somatotrophs, activates Gs‑protein signalling, elevates cAMP, and triggers PKA‑mediated growth hormone release.[web:143][web:322][web:324]

Short half-life, pulsatile profile

The approximate 30‑minute plasma half‑life reported for this analog allows clear GH peaks and returns toward baseline within a few hours, facilitating repeated pulse protocols.[web:152][web:322][web:324]

Key research application areas

1. GH pulse and GH/IGF‑1 axis studies

Used to characterise pulsatile GH secretion, IGF‑1 responses, and feedback regulation in endocrine models.[web:319][web:323][web:324]

2. Combination studies with GHRPs

Often combined with ghrelin‑mimetic peptides (e.g., GHRP‑2, GHRP‑6, ipamorelin) to investigate synergistic GH release through dual‑pathway activation.[web:143][web:322][web:324]

3. Metabolic and body‑composition research

Applied in cell and animal models to explore GH‑linked effects on lipolysis, protein synthesis, and energy balance, always as an experimental compound.[web:319][web:323]

Handling, reconstitution, and storage

Unopened vials

  • Store at −20 °C or below in a dry, dark environment.[web:319][web:321][web:323]
  • Protect from moisture and avoid frequent temperature changes.

Reconstitution

  • Reconstitute with sterile or bacteriostatic water, or an appropriate buffer, under aseptic conditions.[web:319][web:321]
  • Add solvent slowly along the vial wall and swirl gently until fully dissolved.

Reconstituted solutions

  • Store at 2–8 °C for short‑term use, protected from light.[web:319][web:321]
  • For longer storage, aliquot and freeze at −20 °C or below to limit freeze–thaw cycles.

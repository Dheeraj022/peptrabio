# Fragment 176-191 (10mg)

Fragment 176-191 is a synthetic peptide representing the C-terminal portion of human growth hormone (hGH), often called the "lipolytic fragment." It is studied for its potential to influence fat metabolism while avoiding some of the broader effects of full hGH.

## What is Fragment 176-191?
Fragment 176-191 is a small piece of hGH that has been heavily researched in animal models, particularly for its fat-burning properties. Lab studies in mice have shown it can enhance fat breakdown without raising insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1) levels or affecting carbohydrate metabolism and insulin sensitivity like full hGH does [web:65][web:66]. This targeted profile makes it useful for exploring fat metabolism and could inform future anti-obesity research.

## Research Highlights
Research has explored Fragment 176-191 in several areas:

### Blood Sugar Regulation
Animal studies suggest the C-terminal end of hGH, particularly Fragment 176-191, contributes to blood sugar-lowering effects through increased insulin levels. It appears more effective than other hGH fragments for this purpose [web:65].

### Fat Burning and Weight Loss
The peptide earned its "lipolytic" name from mouse studies showing substantial fat loss in obese models. This effect is linked to beta-3 adrenergic receptors and increased energy expenditure, leading to nearly 50% less weight gain over three weeks. Lean mice maintained normal weight, suggesting selective action in obesity [web:66].

### Cartilage Regeneration
A 2015 study in rabbits found weekly injections enhanced cartilage growth, especially when combined with hyaluronic acid. It also reduced osteoarthritis-related disability, raising interest in joint health research [web:66].

### Safety Profile
A 2013 meta-analysis of six phase IIb trials found no changes in glucose tolerance, insulin sensitivity, IGF-1, or adverse events compared to placebo, suggesting it avoids hGH's side effects like insulin resistance or edema [web:66].

Scientific overview

HGH Fragment 176‑191 is a C‑terminal segment of human growth hormone that is used in research to study fat‑metabolism pathways and domain‑specific peptide activity.[web:363][web:367][web:368]

By isolating amino acids 176–191, researchers can explore C‑terminal effects on adipose tissue and energy balance without working with full‑length hGH.[web:360][web:363][web:371]

Mechanistic themes in research

Adipocyte lipid metabolism

Preclinical work explores how Fragment 176‑191 may influence lipolysis, anti‑lipogenesis, and beta‑3 adrenergic–linked signalling cascades in adipose tissue models.[web:360][web:361][web:368]

Domain-specific mapping within hGH

Studies often compare fragment activity with full‑length hGH and analogs such as AOD‑9604 to help map which C‑terminal residues align with fat‑metabolism versus growth‑promoting profiles.[web:358][web:363][web:372]

Key research application areas

1. Fat-metabolism and adipose models

Used to investigate triglyceride breakdown, fatty‑acid release, and potential inhibition of new fat synthesis in adipocyte cultures and animal models.[web:360][web:363][web:371]

2. Energy-balance and weight-gain studies

Applied in diet‑induced or genetic obesity models to follow trends in body weight and fat mass while monitoring broader metabolic markers.[web:358][web:371]

3. hGH structure–activity research

Supports structure–function studies comparing Fragment 176‑191, AOD‑9604, and full‑length hGH in endocrine and peptide‑signalling research.[web:363][web:368][web:372]

Handling, reconstitution, and storage

Unopened vials

  • Store at −20 °C or below in a dry, dark environment.[web:364][web:367]
  • Limit repeated temperature cycling and keep vials tightly sealed.

Reconstitution

  • Reconstitute with sterile or bacteriostatic water, or an appropriate buffer, using aseptic technique.[web:364][web:368]
  • Add solvent slowly along the vial wall and swirl gently until fully dissolved.

Reconstituted solutions

  • Store at 2–8 °C for short‑term use, protected from light.[web:364][web:367]
  • For longer storage, aliquot and freeze at −20 °C or below to reduce repeated freeze–thaw cycles.
  • Discard any solution that becomes cloudy, discoloured, or develops visible particles.

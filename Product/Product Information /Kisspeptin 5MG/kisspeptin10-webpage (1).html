Product overview

Kisspeptin-10 is the smallest fully active fragment in the kisspeptin family and is commonly used to study signaling pathways connected to reproductive regulation. It is valued in research because it offers a direct way to examine KISS1 receptor activity and how that activity connects to upstream neuroendocrine control.

The peptide is also relevant in broader investigative areas including endocrine timing, metastasis-related signaling, placental biology, and behavior-linked neurocircuit research.

**Last updated:** 22 April 2026

PeptraBio (“PeptraBio”, “we”, “us”, “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, store, and protect information when you visit our website, place an order, contact us, or use our services.

This policy is written for customers in India and for international visitors and buyers. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at **info@peptrabio.com**.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

### Information you provide to us
When you interact with our website, place an order, create an account, or contact us, we may collect details such as:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Billing and shipping address
- Company name, laboratory name, or business details, where applicable
- Account login details, if you create an account
- Order history and order-related communication
- Messages sent through forms, email, or customer support
- Newsletter or marketing preferences

### Information collected automatically
When you browse our website, we may automatically collect technical and usage information such as:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device type and operating system
- Pages viewed and time spent on pages
- Referring page or website
- Approximate location based on IP address
- Cookie data, local storage, and similar technologies

### Sensitive information
We do not intentionally ask for protected health information, government ID numbers, or full payment card details. Payments are handled by our payment partners, and we do not store complete card information on our servers.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including business, legal, accounting, tax, and security requirements.

- Account and order records: kept while your account is active and for as long as needed for legal or business reasons
